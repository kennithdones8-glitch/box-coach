BoxCoach

A boxing coach in your phone. Prop it up, train, and it watches, counts and coaches.

Open BoxCoach

What it does

  • Counts and reads your punches with the camera: jab, cross, hooks, uppercuts.
  • Coaches your form out loud: hands up, move your feet, move your head.
  • Builds your session: tap Coach me, say how long you have, and it plans the rounds from what it has seen you do.
  • Shows your progress: guard, output, punch mix and habits over weeks.
  • Learns you: a 3-minute punch test teaches it how your punches look from your camera spot.
  • Defense drills: it calls slip, roll or block and checks you did it.
  • Train with friends: share your stats and see each other side by side.

Private by design

  • No account, no sign-up, free.
  • The camera is read on your phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share it.
  • Your sessions live on your phone (back them up in Settings).

Install it (30 seconds)

  1. Tap Open BoxCoach above first.
  2. iPhone (Safari): tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Android (Chrome): tap ⋮, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

It then opens full screen like any app, and works offline after the first camera session.

First time

  1. Answer four quick questions.
  2. Phone at chest height, 2–3 m away, whole body in view, light in front of you.
  3. Do the punch test: it calls 10 of each punch, then shows how well it read you.
